THE ONE WITH THE RULES

A little friendly competition.

  1. Gather around one screen. Choose up to six teams, or play with no scores. Nominate a host to read clues and judge answers.
  2. Pick any of the three boards. Each has five categories with clues worth $100–$500. Values are points, not real money.
  3. Say your answer out loud. Use a raised hand or your own buzzers. “What is…?” is optional. Agree on partial credit and who answers first before you start.
  4. Reveal, then score. Award the clue to the correct team. If penalties are enabled, mark incorrect teams first. “No one got it” closes the clue without an award.
  5. Keep the night moving. A timer is optional, and Undo reopens the last completed clue and restores its scores. Progress saves in this browser.

Play one board for a shorter game, or all 75 clues for the full couch championship. This is shared-screen play; phone buzzers and online rooms aren’t included.
