# Clue editing notes

The first two boards remix ten categories into two new 25-clue boards. Wording was clarified, the missing quote speaker was supplied, and an unrelated two-part clue was replaced with one coherent question. Category art no longer reveals individual answers. The number category was renamed to avoid giving away its first answer.

The third board has five new categories: careers, travel, family, music, and apartment life. Episode references appear after revealing each new answer. These are host aids, not required player responses. Accept natural spelling variants, including Alessandro's / Allesandro's.
